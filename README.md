# jlawcordova-reel
Tooling to generate video content where the script, video and music are in-sync.

- [`cli/`](cli/README.md): `pixelreel`, the pipeline from storyboard to rendered video.
- [`tooling/cozy-score/`](tooling/cozy-score/README.md): background music that follows a video plan.
- [`intents/`](intents/): intents, specs and plans for planned work.
